Roam3DS

A 3D open-world driving and police pursuit game for the Nintendo 3DS, inspired by Need for Speed: Most Wanted (2012). Cruise freely through an infinite city, smash into traffic, and outrun escalating police chases.

Controls

A = Accelerate
B = Reverse
Y = Look Behind
X = Change Map View
L = Zoom Map Out
R = Zoom Map In
Select = Repair
Circle Pad = Turning
Start = Exit

Features

• Infinite Open City: Free-roam through an endless 3D city with no map borders.
• 5-Star Police Heat System (Currently being refined):
	• Heat Meter: Located at the top-left of the bottom screen. Gain heat by speeding over ~130 km/h (gradual, capped at 30), hitting police (+8), wrecking a police car (+12), or ramming civilian traffic (+18).
	• Escalating Pursuits: Police cruisers spawn on surrounding roads with flashing red and blue lights—up to one cop per star. Higher heat levels increase police top speed, and at 3 stars or more, cruisers aggressively ram your vehicle.
	• Cooldown & Escape: Get more than 100 m away from police to enter Cooldown and drain your heat back to zero.
	• Busted: Stopping within 8 m of a police car for 2.5 seconds triggers BUSTED, resetting your heat and ending the chase.
	• Wreck Counter: Track every police takedown right next to the heat meter.
